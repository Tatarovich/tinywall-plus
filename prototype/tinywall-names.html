TinyWall · name studies

Keep the name. Add a mark.

The exact word TinyWall stays at the start — so search still finds it.
Search rule: unusual letters inside the word (TinyWalll, TinyyWall) break exact matching. A symbol after the word keeps the prefix intact.
Recommended
TinyWall°
A quiet degree mark. Minimal, memorable, and it reads as a design signature rather than a typo.
exact prefix · clean logo
TinyWall·
The softest option: a middle dot, almost a pause. Very quiet, but easy to miss at small sizes.
minimal · subtle
TinyWall⌂
A tiny house: home screen + wall. The most on-theme mark, but depends on font support.
home mark · thematic
TinyWall✦
A four-point star. More decorative and a little louder; works well as an icon accent.
star · decorative
TinyWall+
The familiar plus. Highly searchable and readable, but less unique — many apps use “+”.
familiar · less unique
TinyWall TV
A descriptor instead of a symbol: best discovery for “TV launcher”, less of a design signature.
searchable · descriptive
